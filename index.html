Madurai Travel & Stay

Key details for our beloved guests traveling to the cultural capital of Tamil Nadu.

Transit

Reaching Madurai

Madurai Airport (IXM) offers direct flights from Chennai, Bengaluru, Mumbai, and Delhi, and is only 20 minutes from Thiruparankundram. Madurai Junction (MDU) is connected by Vande Bharat and superfast trains across India.

Stay

Recommended Hotels

Special guest accommodations have been arranged at Heritage Madurai, Courtyard by Marriott Madurai, and Fortune Pandiyan Hotel. Mention ANNAMALAI-SARMILA-WEDDING for family rates.

Sightseeing

Madurai Highlights

While here, immerse in Madurai's rich heritage: visit the world-famous Arulmigu Meenakshi Amman Temple, the historic Thirumalai Nayakkar Mahal, and savor authentic Madurai Jigarthanda and Chettinad filter coffee!
